3. Ordering, cutoff times & order minimums

  • Breakfast orders must be placed before 7:00 AM on the day of delivery.
  • Lunch orders must be placed before 9:00 AM on the day of delivery.
  • Dinner orders must be placed before 4:00 PM on the day of delivery.
  • Breakfast Minimum Food Value: A minimum food order value of ₹40 per day applies to Breakfast orders in standard delivery areas. This minimum is waived for Free Delivery zones (Bhosale Nagar, Triveni Nagar), Self Pickup, or if an active breakfast delivery is already scheduled to your exact flat address on that date.
  • Lunch & Dinner: There is no minimum food order value for Lunch or Dinner.
  • The kitchen is closed on Sundays and on dates marked unavailable by the administration (e.g., scheduled festival breaks).

4. Pricing, delivery charges & fees

  • All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) and include all applicable taxes.
  • Delivery Charges: Standard delivery charge is ₹12 per meal. Delivery becomes completely FREE when the day's food total reaches ₹117 (ordering 1 meal), ₹175 (2 meals), or ₹209 (3 meals). Delivery is always free for Bhosale Nagar, Triveni Nagar, Ganga Serio, and Self Pickup.
  • Small Cart Fee: A small ₹12 cart fee applies to any Lunch or Dinner meal whose food subtotal is below ₹59.
  • Prices, item availability, and delivery charges are clearly displayed on the bill summary before payment confirmation.

5. Payments & On-Account billing

Payment is collected at the time of placing the order via:

  • Svaadh Wallet: Prepaid balance tied to your verified mobile number for 1-tap instant checkout.
  • HDFC SmartGateway: Secure online payment via bank-account UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.), debit/credit cards, or NetBanking. Note: Credit-linked UPI and prepaid wallets (PPI) are not supported. If prompted by your browser, please allow pop-up windows to complete authorization.
  • Split Payment: Automatically uses available Svaadh Wallet balance first, collecting any remaining amount via HDFC SmartGateway.
  • On-Account (Approved Regulars): Approved regular customers may place orders On Account. Monthly on-account statements must be settled before the 3rd of the following month via the gateway (a review reminder is provided on the 1st and 2nd; hard-blocking of new orders begins on the 3rd until previous dues are settled). Daily on-account accounts have a 7-day payment window.
