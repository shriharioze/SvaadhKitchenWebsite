Svaadh Kitchen

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Service areas & storefronts

Svaadh Kitchen provides home-cooked vegetarian meal delivery across targeted zones in Pune:

  • Hadapsar Main Hub: Serving major societies and residential hubs across Hadapsar (including Bhosale Nagar, Triveni Nagar, Magarpatta City, Amanora Park Town, Sasane Nagar, Malwadi, and nearby areas). The full list of selectable areas is provided during checkout.
  • Ganga Serio Storefront (Kharadi): Dedicated storefront (Liviano-Serio) serving Ganga Serio residents in Kharadi with free doorstep delivery for Lunch and Dinner.

2. Free delivery zones

Delivery is always 100% FREE with no minimum order threshold for:

  • Bhosale Nagar
  • Triveni Nagar
  • Ganga Serio, Kharadi (via the Liviano-Serio storefront)
  • Self Pickup (from our Hadapsar or Kharadi kitchens)

3. Standard delivery charges & free thresholds

For all other delivery areas:

  • Standard Delivery Fee: A flat delivery charge of ₹12 per meal applies.
  • Free Delivery Threshold: Delivery becomes completely FREE for the entire day when your day's food subtotal reaches:
    • ₹117 when ordering 1 meal that day
    • ₹175 when ordering 2 meals that day
    • ₹209 when ordering 3 meals that day
  • Small Cart Fee: A small cart fee of ₹12 applies to Lunch or Dinner meals with a food subtotal under ₹59.
  • Breakfast Minimum Food Value: Breakfast delivery orders in standard delivery areas require a minimum food subtotal of ₹40 per day. This minimum is waived for Free Delivery zones (Bhosale Nagar, Triveni Nagar), Self Pickup, or if an active breakfast delivery is already scheduled to your exact flat address on that date.

4. Delivery timings

  • Breakfast: typically 8:30 AM – 9:45 AM
  • Lunch: typically 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM
  • Dinner: typically 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Exact delivery times depend on distance and delivery route sequence. You can track your order status in real time (e.g., "🚗 Out for Delivery") inside Manage Orders.

5. Self Pickup & Porter courier options

  • Hadapsar Kitchen: A 104, Shree Laxmi Vihar Society, Bhosale Nagar, Hadapsar, Pune 411028.
  • Ganga Serio (Kharadi): G2 804, Ganga Serio, Kharadi, Pune.
  • Delivery Slot Caps & Porter: On high-demand days when delivery slots fill up, you can still place your order by choosing Self Pickup (free) or booking a Porter courier to collect your meal directly from our kitchen counter.

6. Order cutoff times

  • Breakfast — before 7:00 AM on the day of delivery
  • Lunch — before 9:00 AM on the day of delivery
  • Dinner — before 4:00 PM on the day of delivery

The kitchen is closed on Sundays and during scheduled festive breaks (such as Diwali Break).

7. Handover & missed deliveries

Our delivery partner will hand over the food package at your doorstep or society gate as selected during ordering. If our delivery partner is unable to contact you on your registered phone number or gain entry, the parcel will be held at the kitchen counter. Please contact us immediately if you encounter any delay.

8. Contact & delivery assistance

For any questions regarding active deliveries or addresses, please WhatsApp us at +91 93222 46765 or call our team at +91 99307 48908.
