Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Order cancellation window

You can cancel an upcoming order free of charge at any time before the kitchen cutoff for that specific meal:

  • Breakfast — before 7:00 AM on the day of delivery
  • Lunch — before 9:00 AM on the day of delivery
  • Dinner — before 4:00 PM on the day of delivery

Once the cutoff time passes, fresh ingredients are allocated and preparation begins in the kitchen; cancellations are strictly not permitted after cutoff.

2. How to cancel an order

Open the order page → tap your profile avatar (initials) in the top-right corner → tap Manage Orders → locate the order in your Upcoming list → tap Delete. After confirmation, the automated cancellation and refund flow executes immediately.

3. Refund methods & timelines

Refund processing depends on the payment method used for that order:

3.1 Orders paid via Svaadh Wallet

  • The full meal amount is instantly credited back to your Svaadh Wallet.
  • The balance is immediately ready to use for future orders.
  • Wallet credits cannot be withdrawn to a bank account or redeemed for physical cash.

3.2 Orders paid via UPI / Card / NetBanking (HDFC SmartGateway)

When you delete an order paid online, you can choose where the refund is directed:

  • Credit to Svaadh Wallet: Processed instantly, giving you immediate 1-tap ordering power on your next meal.
  • Refund to original bank source: Initiated automatically via HDFC SmartGateway back to your original UPI VPA or payment card. Typically settled within 1–2 working days (exact settlement timing depends on your bank).

3.3 Split payment orders (Wallet + Gateway)

The portion paid via Svaadh Wallet returns instantly to your wallet. For the gateway-paid portion, you can choose instant wallet credit or original source refund.

💡 Note on Wallet Recharges: Funds loaded directly into Svaadh Wallet via online recharge are designated strictly for food ordering on Svaadh Kitchen and cannot be withdrawn to external bank accounts.

4. Bulk Plans — Rescheduling (Postpone) & Cancellation

  • Free Postpone (Reschedule): Instead of losing your bulk commitment discount, you can postpone individual days for free! You can postpone up to 2 lunch + 2 dinner days on the 15-Day plan, and up to 4 lunch + 4 dinner days on the Month plan, to any available working day within 30 days without extra charge.
  • Bulk Cancellation: You can cancel any bulk day before its cutoff. Because bulk plans receive upfront commitment discounts (5% for Week, 7.5% for 15-Day, 10% for Month), cancelling a day claws back that day's bulk discount, and the remaining net balance is credited instantly to your Svaadh Wallet.

5. Kitchen-closed days & administrative shifts

If the kitchen pauses operations for a specific meal or full day (such as festive holidays or unforeseen closures):

  • Standard Orders: All affected orders are automatically cancelled with a 100% full refund (wallet orders credit back to wallet immediately; gateway orders are queued for automated gateway refund).
  • Bulk Plan Orders: Affected bulk days are automatically rescheduled to your next available non-order working day. This administrative shift does not count against your personal postpone quota.

6. Quality complaints & issues

We take pride in cooking fresh, wholesome homestyle meals. If an order arrives damaged, missing items, or with a quality concern, please notify us within 2 hours of delivery via WhatsApp at +91 93222 46765 with photos of the food and packaging. We will investigate immediately and issue a replacement or appropriate refund.

If you have any questions or require assistance regarding a refund, please message our support team on WhatsApp at +91 93222 46765 or email svaadh.kitchen@gmail.com with your order ID.
