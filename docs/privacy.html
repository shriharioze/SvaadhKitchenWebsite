Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Svaadh Kitchen ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website (svaadhkitchen.in) and our ordering storefronts, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity & contact: Name, mobile phone number, delivery address (wing, flat, floor, society, area, landmark), and optional email address.
  • Order & transaction data: Items ordered, quantities, dates, dietary/kitchen instructions, payment method chosen, and delivery fulfillment status.
  • Payment data: Processed exclusively through our payment partner, HDFC SmartGateway (Juspay). We do not collect or store sensitive payment information such as debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or bank login credentials. We only retain the transaction reference ID, order ID, payment status, and amount.
  • Technical & device data: Basic device/browser information required to securely render the order interface and support the Progressive Web App (PWA).

2. How we use your information

  • To receive, prepare, dispatch, and deliver your food orders accurately.
  • To communicate real-time order updates, delivery partner details, and important service notifications via WhatsApp, SMS, or in-app notices.
  • To process cancellations, refunds, and address customer support queries.
  • To maintain Svaadh Wallet prepaid balances and On-Account ledgers tied to your verified phone number.
  • To continuously improve our kitchen operations, menu planning, and delivery efficiency (analyzed in aggregated, non-personally identifiable form).

3. Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted partners necessary for service delivery:

  • HDFC SmartGateway (Juspay): To securely authorize and process digital payments and refunds.
  • Our Delivery Partners: Customer name, delivery address, phone number, and gate entry codes solely to complete the physical delivery of your order.
  • Legal / Regulatory Authorities: Only when strictly required by applicable Indian laws or statutory obligations.

4. Data retention

Order and customer transaction records are retained for as long as required to maintain account history, comply with statutory tax and accounting standards, and resolve disputes. Svaadh Wallet records remain active until refunded or account closure is requested.

5. Security

Customer data is stored in access-controlled environments. All payment transactions occur over encrypted HTTPS connections directly on HDFC SmartGateway's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. Please keep your account PIN and OTPs strictly confidential; Svaadh Kitchen staff will never ask for your PIN or banking passwords.

6. Your choices & rights

  • You may review or update your saved delivery addresses directly in the app drawer at any time.
  • You can request a summary of the personal data we maintain for your account.
  • You can request correction or deletion of your profile data by writing to svaadh.kitchen@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 93222 46765.
  • You may opt out of promotional messages at any time.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use essential browser local storage strictly to keep you logged in across sessions, preserve your active cart, and save your preferred delivery details. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Children's privacy

Our ordering service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest revision date will always be reflected at the top of this page.

10. Grievances & contact

For any privacy inquiries or grievance redressal, please contact our team at svaadh.kitchen@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 93222 46765.
